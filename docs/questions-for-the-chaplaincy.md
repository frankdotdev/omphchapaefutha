# Questions for the Chaplaincy Office

This document records the specific local details needed from the Chaplaincy Office to complete the chaplaincy and society profiles for production. While the wider movement histories and Catholic spiritualities have been researched and drafted in `database/seeds/0003_society_details.sql`, local facts cannot be assumed and must be supplied by the chaplaincy.

---

## 1. Chaplaincy History & General Information

1. **Founding & Origins:**
   - In what year did Catholic chaplaincy services begin at the hospital (Alex Ekwueme Federal University Teaching Hospital, Abakaliki / AE-FUTHA)?
   - Under which Diocesan Bishop was the chaplaincy established, and by whose initiative?
   - Where was the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass first celebrated before the current church/chapel was built?

2. **Past Chaplains & Ecclesiastical Appointments:**
   - Names and tenure years of past Chaplains and Assistant Chaplains who served here.
   - Any resident religious sisters or consecrated communities serving the chaplaincy or hospital wards.

3. **Chapel Building & Milestones:**
   - When was the cornerstone of the current chaplaincy building laid and blessed?
   - Date and prelate of formal dedication/consecration of the chapel altar.
   - Significant building, renovation, or expansion milestones.

4. **Patronal Feast Celebration:**
   - How is the annual patronal feast of Our Mother of Perpetual Help (27 June) celebrated locally? (e.g. novena schedule, procession routes through the hospital grounds).

5. **Infant Baptism & Sacraments:**
   - Exact clock time for Infant Baptism on the third Saturday of each month.
   - Regular schedule for Holy Confession and Catechism classes.

6. **Office & Contact Information:**
   - Official office physical address on hospital grounds.
   - Official phone numbers and email addresses for pastoral inquiries.
   - Bank details for Mass intention offerings and chaplaincy support (Bank name, Account name, Account number).
   - Standard recommended Mass stipend / intention offering amount.

## 2. Society Local Details

For each of the 19 associations, organizations, and devotional societies listed below, the chaplaincy office should supply:
- **Local founding year** at AE-FUTHA / FETHA.
- **Current meeting schedule** (day of week, time of day).
- **Meeting venue** (e.g., Chapel hall, Classroom block, Ward common room).
- **Membership process** (how a student, doctor, nurse, or parishioner joins).
- **Current executive officers** (President, Secretary, Spiritual Director/Animator).

### Associations
1. **Altar Knights Association:**
   - Local meeting and practice schedule.
   - Requirements for new altar servers (age/training duration).
2. **Choir:**
   - Weekly rehearsal days and times.
   - Sections (senior choir, youth/student choir).
3. **Church Wardens:**
   - Meeting schedule and uniform/roster requirements.
4. **Altar Decorators:**
   - Cleaning and sanctuary preparation roster days.
5. **Board of Lectors:**
   - Training, probation, and audition requirements for new readers.
   - Meeting days for lectionary study.

### Organizations
6. **Catholic Men Organization (CMO):**
   - Monthly meeting Sunday/time and venue.
   - Dues and local welfare programs.
7. **Catholic Women Organization (CWO):**
   - Monthly meeting Sunday/time and annual Mothering Sunday traditions.
   - Home and hospital visitation schedule.
8. **Catholic Youth Organization of Nigeria (CYON):**
   - Regular youth fellowship meeting day and time.
   - Annual youth week dates and programs.
9. **Nigerian Federation of Catholic Students (NFCS):**
   - Campus meeting days and venue.
   - Welcome orientation schedule for incoming clinical and medical students.
10. **Federation of Catholic Medical and Dental Students (FECAMDS):**
    - Clinical ward prayer and fellowship schedule.
    - Mentorship program between doctors/consultants and medical students.
11. **Knights of Saint John (KSJ / LAUX):**
    - Local Commandery and Ladies Auxiliary details.
    - Meeting dates and chaplaincy functions.

### Societies and Devotional Groups
12. **Legion of Mary:**
    - Name(s) of local praesidia (e.g., Praesidium Our Lady Mediatrix of All Graces).
    - Praesidium meeting day, time, and room.
13. **Our Mother of Perpetual Help Devotional Society:**
    - Weekly novena day and time at the chaplaincy.
    - Feast day novena structure.
14. **Rosa Mystica:**
    - Weekly prayer meeting and Hour of Grace observance details.
15. **Block Rosary Crusade:**
    - Local centre location(s) on hospital grounds / quarters.
    - Evening gathering time for children.
16. **Sacred Heart of Jesus Devotion:**
    - First Friday adoration schedule and Thursday Holy Hour time.
17. **Hospital Visitationaries of Mary:**
    - Ward visitation days and assigned wards.
    - Protocol for notifying the chaplain on call.
18. **Saint Anthony of Padua Society:**
    - Tuesday novena time and Saint Anthony's Bread distribution program.
19. **Catholic Charismatic Renewal of Nigeria (CCRN):**
    - Weekly prayer meeting day, time, and venue (e.g., Praise & Worship, teachings).
    - Ministries active in the prayer group (Intercessory, Evangelical, Greeting, etc.).
